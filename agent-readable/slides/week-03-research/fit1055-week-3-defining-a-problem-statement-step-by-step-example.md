## Slide 9: Defining Problem Statements

- Defining Problem Statements
- FIT1055 IT Professional Practice and Ethics | Monash University
- 9 / 17
- The integrated sources will be further analyzed to develop strong problem statements. A problem statement is a clear, concise description of an issue or challenge that needs to be addressed. It defines how the system should ideally behave, what the problem is, why it matters, who it affects, what issues may arise after implementation, and what the desired outcome should be. Think of it as the foundation that guides your entire research or project—it tells you exactly what you're trying to solve.

## Slide 10: Defining Problem Statements

- Defining Problem Statements
- The How–What–Why Framework
- FIT1055 IT Professional Practice and Ethics | Monash University
- 10 / 17
- A strong problem statement in AI ethics requires moving from scattered information to structured analysis.
- How
- How should AI systems operate under ideal conditions?
- What
- What are the specific ethical issues that arose from the real-world AI deployment?
- Why
- Why did these ethical issues arise from the AI implementation? What is the root cause?
- A pattern is a high-level concept you define based on recurring themes/incidents highlighted from your sources.

- Pattern 1: Accuracy and Reliability

- Pattern 2: Bias and Discrimination
